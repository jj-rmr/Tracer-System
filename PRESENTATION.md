# User Guide – How to Use the Placement Tracer System

**This guide shows you how to use the app. If you're setting up the app, see QUICK_START.md instead.**

---

## 🎯 What is the Placement Tracer System?

The **Placement Tracer System** is an online application that surveys ParSU graduates about their:

- Employment status and career
- Further education/studies
- Personal and professional outcomes

**Why we use it:**

- Track graduate employment outcomes
- Improve academic programs based on feedback
- Support career services
- Report to accreditation bodies

---

## 👥 Three Types of Users

### 1. Alumni (Graduates)

**What you can do:**

- View open tracer studies
- Fill out the tracer form
- Save your progress as draft
- Upload supporting documents (certificates, IDs, etc.)
- Submit your response when ready
- Update response if study is still open
- Delete your response

### 2. Coordinators (Staff Members)

**What you can do:**

- View responses from your assigned programs
- Search and filter responses
- View full details of each response
- Review uploaded documents
- Add notes to responses
- Enter manual responses for students
- Import batch data from Excel/CSV
- Export data as Excel spreadsheet
- Download files from Google Drive

### 3. Administrators (Management)

**What you can do:**

- Everything coordinators can do
- Create new tracer studies
- View all responses (all programs)
- Manage user accounts and permissions
- Open/close studies
- View audit logs (who did what when)
- Export all data

## 📋 Section A: For Alumni

### How to Complete a Tracer Form

#### Step 1: Find Open Studies

1. Log in to the system
2. Your dashboard shows all available studies
3. Look for studies with status **"Open"**
4. Click on the study you want to respond to

#### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

1. The form has sections:
   - **Personal Information** – Name, email, program, graduation year
   - **Employment Status** – Are you employed, studying, or looking?
   - **Employment Details** – Company, position, salary range (if applicable)
   - **Further Study** – Program, school, field of study (if applicable)
   - **Additional Questions** – Skills, challenges, advice
2. You can:
   - Fill out all at once
   - Save as **draft** and come back later
   - Fill partially and return tomorrow

**Important:** Draft responses are only saved on your account. No one else can see them until you submit.

#### Step 3: Upload Documents (Optional)

Some studies ask for supporting documents:

1. Click **"Upload File"**
2. Select document from your computer (PDF, Word, image, etc.)
3. Wait for upload to complete (shows progress bar)
4. You can upload multiple documents
5. You can delete uploaded files if you change your mind

**Accepted files:** PDF, Word, Excel, images, photos
**Max size:** Usually 10MB per file

#### Step 4: Submit Your Response

1. Review all your answers
2. Click **"Submit"**
3. System verifies all required fields are filled
4. If validation fails, you'll see error messages (red text)
5. Fix errors and try again
6. Once submitted, your response is saved

**After submission:**

- You cannot edit the form unless coordinator asks you to
- You CAN still delete your response
- You can update response if coordinator reopens the study

#### Step 5: Update or Delete Your Response

**Update (if study is reopened):**

1. Open your submitted response
2. Click **"Edit"** (if available)
3. Change your answers
4. Click **"Submit Again"**

**Delete:**

1. Open your response
2. Click **"Delete"**
3. Confirm deletion
4. Response is permanently removed

## 👔 Section B: For Coordinators

### Understanding Your Access

You can only see responses from your **assigned programs**.

**Example:**

- If you're assigned to: Computer Science + Information Technology
- You'll see: All responses from CS and IT students
- You won't see: Engineering or Business responses

### How to View Responses

#### Step 1: Navigate to Responses

1. Log in
2. Click **"Responses"** in menu
3. You'll see a list of all responses in your programs

#### Step 2: Filter and Search

**Filters available:**

- **Study Period** – Select which year/semester
- **Status** – Submitted only, or include drafts
- **Program** – If you manage multiple programs
- **Search** – Find by respondent name or email
- **Date Range** – Find responses from specific dates

**Example:** To find all submitted IT responses from 2025:

1. Select Study: "2025-2026"
2. Select Program: "BS Information Technology"
3. Select Status: "Submitted"
4. Click **"Filter"**

#### Step 3: View Individual Response

1. Click on any response in the list
2. You'll see:
   - All form answers
   - All uploaded documents
   - Submit date and time
   - Previous notes (if any)
3. You can add notes (stored confidentially)
4. You can download any uploaded file

#### Step 4: Edit Response

**When you can edit:**

- Before submission (draft responses)
- After submission (if authorized)
- When importing manual data

**How to edit:**

1. Open response
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Change answers
4. Click **"Save"**
5. Change is recorded with timestamp and your name

### How to Enter Manual Responses

For alumni who can't submit online, coordinators can enter their responses manually.

#### Step 1: Create Manual Response Draft

1. Click **"New Manual Response"**
2. Enter:
   - Respondent name
   - Respondent email (optional)
   - Select program
   - Select study period
3. Click **"Create"**

#### Step 2: Import Data

1. Open the draft response
2. Click **"Import Data"**
3. Upload CSV or Excel file with responses
4. Attach supporting documents (photos, documents)
5. Click **"Import"**

#### Step 3: Review and Organize

1. System validates data
2. Shows any errors
3. You fix errors if needed
4. Click **"Complete"**
5. System organizes files in Google Drive
6. Response becomes part of official data

### How to Export Data

Download responses as Excel spreadsheet.

#### Step 1: Select Criteria

1. Click **"Export"**
2. Choose filters:
   - Study period
   - Program(s)
   - Status (submitted only)
   - Date range (optional)
3. Click **"Generate"**

#### Step 2: Download

1. System creates formatted Excel file
2. Download to your computer
3. Open in Excel
4. Data is ready for analysis

**What you get in Excel:**

- Column headers with question names
- Rows with respondent data
- Formatted for easy reading
- Protected against formula injection
- Includes submission dates

## 🔐 Section C: For Administrators

### Managing Studies

#### Create New Study

1. Click **"Studies"** in admin menu
2. Click **"New Study"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Form Version** – Select which tracer form to use
   - **Academic Year** – e.g., "2025-2026"
   - **Study Title** – e.g., "Graduate Tracer 2025"
   - **Opening Date** – When alumni can start submitting
   - **Closing Date** – When responses stop being accepted
4. Click **"Create"**
5. System creates:
   - Study record
   - Google Drive folders for organization
   - Upload staging area

#### Open/Close Studies

1. Click on study in list
2. Click **"Open"** to allow responses
   - Alumni can now submit
   - Coordinators can review
3. Later, click **"Close"** to stop accepting responses
   - No new responses allowed
   - Existing responses still visible
4. Click **"Archive"** to hide study
   - Kept for historical records

### Managing User Accounts

#### Add New User

1. Click **"Accounts"** in admin menu
2. Click **"Add User"**
3. Enter:
   - Email (must be @parsu.edu.ph)
   - Role: Admin, Coordinator, or Alumni
   - If Coordinator:
     - Select programs they can access
     - Can assign: Campus, College, or Program level
4. Click **"Create"**
5. User receives email with login instructions

#### Change User Role

1. Click on user account
2. Click **"Change Role"**
3. Select new role
4. Confirm
5. Change is logged (who changed, when, why)

#### Assign Program Access (Coordinators)

1. Open coordinator account
2. Click **"Manage Access"**
3. Add programs they can see
4. Example:
   - Campus: Manila
   - College: CIT
   - Program: CS BS
5. Save
6. Coordinator can now see all responses from these programs

#### Deactivate Account

1. Open user account
2. Click **"Deactivate"**
3. User cannot log in
4. Historical data preserved

### View Audit Logs

See all sensitive actions (for compliance):

1. Click **"Audit Logs"** in admin menu
2. View all changes:
   - Who made changes
   - What was changed
   - When it happened
   - What the change was
3. Filter by:
   - Type of action
   - Date range
   - User who made change

**Logged events:**

- User role changes
- Data exports
- Account creation/deletion
- Study creation/deletion
- Manual response imports

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Sign In & Access

**Q: I forgot my password**
A: Click "Forgot password?" on sign-in page. Google will send password reset email to your @parsu.edu.ph account.

**Q: I can't sign in with my email**
A: Your account must be created by administrator. Ask your department for account setup.

**Q: Can I sign in from my phone?**
A: Yes, system works on phones, tablets, and computers.

### For Alumni

**Q: Can I save my response and finish later?**
A: Yes! Click "Save Draft" anytime. Your answers are saved. You can leave and come back whenever.

**Q: Can I update my response after submitting?**
A: Only if:

1. The study is still open
2. Coordinator specifically allows updates
   If the study is closed, you cannot update.

**Q: Can I delete my response?**
A: Yes, you can delete your response anytime, even after submission. Click "Delete" button.

**Q: What if I upload the wrong document?**
A: Click the X next to the file to delete it. Then upload the correct file.

**Q: What file types can I upload?**
A: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), images (JPG, PNG), and most common formats.

### For Coordinators

**Q: Can I see all responses?**
A: Only responses from your assigned programs. If you need access to more programs, ask administrator.

**Q: Can I change respondent answers?**
A: Yes, but the system records who made the change and when.

**Q: How do I export all data?**
A: Click "Export", select your filters, click "Download Excel". All your data downloads as spreadsheet.

**Q: Can I import data from multiple people?**
A: Yes, prepare Excel/CSV file with all responses, then import all at once.

### For Administrators

**Q: How do I create a new study?**
A: Go to Studies → New Study → Fill form → Click Create. System automatically sets up folders.

**Q: What happens if I delete a study?**
A: Study is archived (not completely deleted). You can still view historical data, but it won't show in active studies.

**Q: How do I reset a coordinator's access?**
A: Open their account → "Manage Access" → Update or clear programs → Save.

**Q: Where can I see who exported data?**
A: Go to "Audit Logs" → Filter by event type "export" → See all exports with who, when, and what was exported.

## 🎨 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "You don't have permission to see this"

**Solution:**

- You're trying to view a program you're not assigned to
- Ask administrator to give you access
- Or ask another coordinator who has access

### Issue: "I can't upload files"

**Solution:**

- Check file size (max 10MB usually)
- Check file type (PDF, Word, Excel, images)
- Try again - internet might be slow
- Try different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Ask IT for help

### Issue: "Study won't open"

**Solution:**

- Refresh page (press F5)
- Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- Sign out and sign back in
- Try different browser
- Ask IT if system is down

### Issue: "Can't see my responses"

**Solution:**

- Make sure you're looking at the right study
- Try filtering by study period
- Try search by name/email
- Contact administrator to verify your access

### Issue: "Export didn't work"

**Solution:**

- Try again with fewer filters
- Try exporting from different study
- Check your Downloads folder (file might be there)
- If still not working, contact administrator

## 🎓 Tips & Best Practices

### For Alumni

✅ **Do:**

- Save draft frequently while filling form
- Upload clear photos of documents
- Fill out all required fields (marked with *)
- Review answers before submitting
- Submit well before closing date

❌ **Don't:**

- Wait until last minute to submit
- Upload blurry/illegible documents
- Submit without reviewing
- Share your login with others

### For Coordinators

✅ **Do:**

- Review responses regularly
- Add notes for your records
- Keep accurate submission records
- Archive old studies
- Use consistent naming

❌ **Don't:**

- Change alumni answers without noting why
- Share access credentials
- Export more data than needed
- Store export files insecurely

### For Administrators

✅ **Do:**

- Review audit logs regularly
- Archive old studies annually
- Keep coordinator access current
- Test system before major study
- Document any system changes

❌ **Don't:**

- Delete studies (archive instead)
- Grant unnecessary permissions
- Skip auditing
- Share admin credentials
